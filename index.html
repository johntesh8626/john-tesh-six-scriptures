A note from John

What I clung to when nothing else was working

I was diagnosed with a rare and aggressive cancer. Stage four. The doctors gave me eighteen months to live, and they meant it.

The medicine mattered. So did MD Anderson. So did Connie, who never let me settle into the bed and disappear into the diagnosis.

But there were six scriptures I came back to over and over. At 2 a.m. when the ruminations started. In the bathroom mirror at the hospital. In the elevator on the day I thought I wasn't going to make it home.

These six verses didn't just comfort me. They changed what I believed was possible.

I wrote them down. I added the stories of when each one mattered, and what I believed about God before, during, and after.

It became this little book. Thirteen pages. You can read it in one sitting.

If you're walking through something hard right now — or you love someone who is — I'd like you to have it. There's no charge. Just tell me where to send it.

— John
John Tesh, host of Intelligence for Your Life
